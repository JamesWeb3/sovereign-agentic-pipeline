## Why these two

`sdc-review-request-2026-08` tells us the Southland Sustainable Resources Coalition lodged a
s128 review request on 27 July 2026 under Condition GEN4, on entirely acoustic grounds. The
council summarises that case; it does not reproduce it. The request as lodged is not on the
council's page.

Two reasons it matters:

1. It would be the **first `climate-first` source in the corpus**. Every objector voice
   currently reaches the graph as a quote a journalist selected — see
   `research/sources/climate-first/README.md`.
2. The decision on whether to open the review is due **5 September 2026**, so the window for
   reading the case before it is decided is short.

## Notes on handling the replies

- Twenty working days is the statutory maximum for the LGOIMA request. Sent early September,
  a reply is due early October.
- Anything received becomes a source file in the normal way: frontmatter with a `url` if it
  is published anywhere, otherwise record the request and response as the trace and say so
  explicitly in the file. A document received by email and not published needs a plan for how
  a reader checks it — the honest options are to publish it ourselves or to describe exactly
  how it was obtained.
- A refusal, and the ground relied on, is itself worth a note here. So is silence.
