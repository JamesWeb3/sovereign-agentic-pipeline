## Summary

The document the headline workforce numbers actually come from. Because it has to size car
parks and model peak-hour traffic, it states on-site headcount directly — which no other
source in the corpus does. It is the origin of the 550 construction figure and it gives
three distinct operational numbers that have been reported as if they were one.

## Relevant quotes

Construction workforce, section 7.1:

> "Up to 550 staff may be present on the site during the peak construction period. We have
> assessed the effects of up to 300 light vehicle movements per hour (assuming a degree of
> carpooling, staggering of start and finish times, shuttles etc)."

> "We have assumed that these movements will be split evenly between Flora Road East and
> Taylor Road."

Construction parking, section 5:

> "During peak construction, we have assumed that up to 400 car parks may be required."

Operational workforce, section 7.2:

> "The data centre park will operate 24/7 and will employ between 60 – 100 staff, some of whom
> will be operating on shifts. The following staff are anticipated on site at any one time:
> • Administration staff: 5 staff, working 0800–1700, Monday to Friday
> • Security staff: 10 staff, operating 24/7 in rotating shifts
> • Operations management: 5 staff, working 0800–2200, 7 days a week
> • Operations staff: Up to 26 staff, working variable shifts, 7 days a week
> • Up to 45 staff on site at any one time"

> "Assuming a worst-case scenario where all staff arrive and depart during the same peak hour
> and travel in single-occupancy vehicles, approximately 45 veh/hr and less than 100 veh/day
> are expected during the operational phase."

Operational parking, section 5:

> "Once operational and based on up to 45 staff being on site at any one time, the site will
> require approximately 70 car parks to accommodate administration, operations, security
> staff, and visitors."

Substation staffing:

> "The substation component of the site will not have permanent staff. Maintenance visits will
> occur irregularly, with a typical frequency of 10–30 light vehicle movements per day during
> short-term maintenance periods. In rare cases (approximately once every 20 years), up to 20
> personnel may be on-site for extended maintenance, generating up to 30 light vehicle
> movements per day over a two-month period."

Earthworks phase traffic, Table 7.1:

> "Total heavy vehicle trips 13,500 / Total heavy vehicle movements (two-way) 27,000 / Total
> working days (6 days per week*52 weeks) 312 / Total working hours (12 hours per day*312
> days) 3,744 / Daily heavy vehicle movements (two-way) 87 / Hourly heavy vehicle movements
> (two-way) 7 / Peak hour light vehicle movements (two-way) 300"
> — 12-month earthworks phase

Access arrangements, Executive Summary:

> "Once operational, all site-generated traffic will access the site via Flora Road East, with
> Taylor Road functioning as an emergency-only access. During construction, heavy vehicles
> will access the site via Taylor Road, while light vehicles will use both Flora Road East and
> Taylor Road."
