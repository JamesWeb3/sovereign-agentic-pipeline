## Summary

The applicant's own economic modelling, and the origin of the employment figures that
circulate in coverage of the project. It is the only source in the corpus that states what
kind of quantity its job numbers are: input-output multiplier estimates of national,
economy-wide employment, direct and indirect, not headcount on the Makarewa site.

## Relevant quotes

Construction phase, Executive Summary:

> "Over a three-year construction period, Datagrid's capital spend is expected to:
> • Sustain approximately 5,750 jobs (approximately 5,390 FTEs) per annum;
> • Generate $1.48billion in wages and salaries;
> • Contribute $2.53billion to national GDP; and
> • Deliver an estimated $380million in one-off GST."

> "The largest employment impacts will occur in the electrical equipment, construction
> services, and machinery manufacturing sectors."

Operational phase, Executive Summary:

> "Once operational, the proposal is expected to support around 80 workers (72 FTEs) across
> operations, technical services, and substation maintenance."

Table 4, "One-Time Economic Impacts of the Proposal (spread over 3 years)" — Annual Jobs:

> "Direct impacts 291 / 445 / 2,314 / 3,050
> Indirect impacts 192 / 578 / 1,932 / 2,701
> Total 483 / 1,023 / 4,245 / 5,751"
> — columns: Planning & Design, Infrastructure & Civil Works, Data Centre Construction, Totals

Same table, Annual FTEs totals:

> "429 / 963 / 3,995 / 5,387"

What the categories mean, section 6.2 Methodology:

> "Direct impacts – which capture all onsite and offsite activities directly related to the
> proposal's development. For example, builders and their various subcontractors and
> suppliers, some of which will be onsite, and some of which will be offsite."

> "Indirect effects – which capture additional (supply-chain) impacts arising when businesses
> working directly on the project source goods and services from their suppliers, who in turn
> may need to source goods and services from their own suppliers, and so on."

> "Total FTEs – which equals the total number of full-time equivalent workers employed."

> "Total Jobs – which is the total number of people employed. i.e. including both part-time
> and full-time workers."

Section 6.4 summary:

> "Overall, development of the data centre is estimated to provide full-time work for nearly
> 5,390 people for 3 years, generate nearly $1.5 billion in wages/salaries, and boost GDP by
> more than $2.5 billion."

Where the operational headcount comes from, section 7.2 Methodology:

> "Ongoing employment figures for the data centre park were provided directly by Alfatech and
> Electronet, based on the specific operational requirements of the proposal."

> "Inputting the operational workforce estimates provided by Alfatech for the data centre (an
> average daily workforce of 80 staff) and converting substation maintenance activity
> estimates from Electronet into an annualised 0.5 FTEs."

Table 6, "Estimated Annual Economic Impacts of the Proposal (at full build-out)":

> "Data Centre Operation — Jobs 80.0, FTEs 71.8, GDP $35.8m, Wages $11.7m
> Substation Maintenance — Jobs 0.5, FTEs 0.5, GDP $0.1m, Wages $0.1m
> Totals — Jobs 80.5, FTEs 72.3, GDP $35.9m, Wages $11.8m"

> "In summary, once operational, the proposal could sustain the following activity at full
> build-out: • Full-time employment for approximately 72 people; • Annual GDP of $36 million;
> and • $12 million paid annually in wages/salaries."

## Claims this supports

- `claim-makarewa-jobs-permanent` — 80 jobs / 72.3 FTEs at full build-out, from Alfatech's average daily workforce of 80
- `claim-makarewa-economic-impact` — $2.53b national GDP, $1.48b wages over construction; $36m GDP and $12m wages annually *(new claim id)*
