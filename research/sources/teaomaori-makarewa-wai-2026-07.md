## Relevant quotes and figures

> The facility is "expected to use 220 million litres of Groundwater per year." Cooling is
> by recirculation rather than discharging used water back into the environment.

> Environment Southland assessed the activities as having "no more than minor environmental
> effects", so public notification was not required.

> The application *was* notified to iwi representatives including **Te Rūnaka o Waihōpai**,
> **Te Rūnanga o Ōraka-Aparima**, and **Te Rūnanga o Ngāi Tahu**, plus local councils and
> the Department of Conservation.
