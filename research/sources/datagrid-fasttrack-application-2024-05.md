## Summary

Datagrid's section 2B fast-track application for a data centre park at 370 and 342 Flora
Road East, Makarewa, Southland, plus a submarine cable landing at Oreti Beach and a
dedicated Transpower substation.

## Relevant quotes

> "The Applicant intends to develop a Data Centre Park to attract hyperscale and other
> data centre developers to set up their operations in Southland. The project will
> include: 49ha of land ready for the data centre development, a submarine cable (Te
> Waipounamu) interconnecting Australia and Invercargill; a cable landing station capable
> of serving up to 3 submarine cables; and a dedicated 220kV substation (Transpower GXP)."
> — Section 2, project summary

> "The capacity of such a park could reach over 240MW of IT load."
> — Section 2, project details (stated twice, incl. under component 3)

> "New Zealand power generation is currently 82% renewable with concerted efforts under
