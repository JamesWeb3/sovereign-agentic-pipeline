## Summary

The Southland Sustainable Resources Coalition's formal request that Southland District Council
review the conditions of the Makarewa land use consent under s128 of the RMA. It argues the Marshall
Day Acoustics assessment modelled the wrong primary noise source, assessed no low-frequency noise or
infrasound, and was never independently reviewed, and that written approvals from neighbours were
therefore given on incomplete information. It proposes 14 specific consent conditions.

Two things make it more than a complaint. It states that the coalition **does not seek to undo the
consent** — only to change its conditions. And its central technical claim rests on the applicant's
own account of how the site will be cooled.

### Executive summary, pages 1–2

> "This consent authorises New Zealand's first hyperscale data centre — a 280 MW, NZ$3.5 billion
> facility on 49 hectares at Makarewa, more than 12 times larger than any existing data centre in the
> country and more than doubling the entire national data processing capacity. It is located in a
> General Rural Zone with night-time background noise levels as low as 26 dB LA90 — among the
> quietest environments in which any industrial facility has ever been assessed in New Zealand."

> "The acoustic assessment undertaken by Marshall Day Acoustics ("MDA"), supporting the application
> contains fundamental technical deficiencies that may have materially influenced the decision to
> grant consent on a non-notified basis, including:
> • Incorrect primary noise source identification — free air cooling fans (actual continuous source,
> >80% runtime) were omitted while chillers (backup source, <20% runtime) were modelled as continuous
> • No low-frequency noise (LFN, 20–200 Hz) or infrasound (<20 Hz) assessment despite multiple
> LFN-generating sources
> • No independent third-party peer review was commissioned at any stage
> • Ground Factor for sound propagation was unspecified despite geotechnical confirmation of alluvial
> soils with shallow groundwater
> • Insufficient detail on HV substation equipment (only "2 transformers" modelled for a 4-hectare
> substation)
> • Baseline monitoring was inadequate (7 days, single position, A-weighted **[obscured]** season)
> • Building envelope break-out analysis was absent
> • Generator maintenance testing not modelled as a separate operational scenario"

On written approvals:

> "The consent was granted on 11 March 2026 on a non-notified basis under Section 95 of the RMA.
> Written approvals were obtained from neighbouring property owners based solely on the MDA Report's
> A-weighted predictions, and the Council's planner adopted the report's findings directly without
> independent verification."

What the coalition is and is not asking for:

> "We acknowledge the consent was granted lawfully under the procedures followed at the time and do

> "The proposed conditions are consistent with practices already applied by Auckland Council
