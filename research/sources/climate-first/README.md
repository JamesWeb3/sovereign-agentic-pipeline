# climate-first

Sources arguing from environmental, climate or resource-protection grounds, speaking in their
own voice.

## Why this folder was empty until 2026-09-03

The reason is structural, not a gap in searching. **Both consents were non-notified**, so
there was no submission process. The mechanism that normally produces a body of objector
documents — public submissions on a notified application — never ran. See
`../official/es-consent-water-2026` and `../official/sdc-s104-decision-2026-03`, each of
which disposes of notification in a single sentence.

The consequence for the corpus: every climate-first document sits *outside* the consent
process, in party policy, petitions, and the s128 review request. And until one is filed
here, every objector voice in the graph is a quote a journalist chose to print — which the
evidence-role line marks as secondary.

## What is here

- `greens-pause-ai-data-centres-2026-07` — Green Party call for a one-year moratorium,
  26 July 2026. Names the Makarewa consent and puts its electricity demand at about six per
  cent of national supply.

- `greens-national-policy-response-2026-08` — the Greens' response to the government's 14 August 2026 data centre proposal.
- `greens-pause-petition-2026` — the petition text behind the pause call, with three proposed rules.
- `te-tangi-a-tauira-2008` — the Ngāi Tahu ki Murihiku iwi management plan; its water-take policies tested against the Makarewa consent.
- `ssrc-s128-request-2026-07` — the coalition's full s128 request, 26 July 2026, signed by Michelle Hayward. The objectors' case in their own words, with 14 proposed conditions.
- `ssrc-s128-review-form-2026-08` — the Southland Sustainable Resource Coalition's own template s128 submission, from its website. The first coalition document in the corpus.
