By Alexia Russell, 17 March 2026, 9:55am.

## Summary

RNZ's account of the approved Makarewa facility, published the day after approval. The
useful part of this source is the physical detail (halls, barriers, generators) that does
not appear in the application summary, and the mayor on the record defending the
consultation process.

## Relevant quotes

> "It will be the country's second biggest user of electricity after the Tiwai Point
> aluminium smelter."

> "12-metre-high noise control barriers over 9.5 hectares on a 48-ha property"

> "There will be 84 emergency generators, each with a 10,000 litre diesel tank and a 15m
> high exhaust stack."

> "with up to 550 workers expected to be on site"

> "it will only require about 50 staff to keep it going"

> "Fast Track approval has just been given for a large Contact Energy wind farm just 50
> kilometres away"

Rob Scott, Southland mayor:

> "these people have done it right."

> "They've talked to people, they've consulted the community, but more importantly,
> they've listened. They've taken account of the things that they've said, and they've
> tried to change things."

> "Most of the people who live around them have given their written approval for what's
> going on."
