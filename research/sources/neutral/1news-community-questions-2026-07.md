meeting.

## Relevant quotes

> "A $3.5b project has been approved in Makarewa, 10 minutes north of Invercargill, with
> locals wanting answers."

> "Datagrid said construction will create 1200 jobs but when the centre is fully open,
> only 80 staff will be needed."

> "The centre is expected to open in 2028."

> "Today, Mercury also announced it bought a 12% stake in Datagrid for $53 million."

> "Datagrid and Mercury also signed a 140MW long-term power purchase agreement in March."

Kelly Blomfield, Southland Sustainable Resource Coalition organiser:

> "As we come across more information, we're going to have more evenings like this and
> opportunities for people to have their say."

> "We've reached out in good faith and it's a shame but they might come to the next one."

Sam Stubbs, Simplicity managing director:

> "What we have seen with data centres now, and there's a lot of evidence, is that they
> employ very few people, they pay very low taxes, they're masters at paying the lowest
> possible taxes but they consume a huge amount of power and the last thing New
> Zealanders need right now is higher power bills."

Rob Scott, Southland Mayor:

> "The technology at the moment with AI and everything else is moving in a pace that the
> world can't actually keep up with. And I think we're getting a consequence of that now.
> So we're all trying to work within the rules and the regulations that we've got, and
> sometimes, they need to be challenged."
