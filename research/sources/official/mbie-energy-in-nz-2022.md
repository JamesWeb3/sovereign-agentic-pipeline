## Summary

The document the Datagrid fast-track application's "82% renewable" figure comes from. MBIE
records the 2021 renewable share of electricity generation as **82.1 per cent** — the only
published New Zealand figure that matches the application's claim, and one edition out of
date by the time the application was filed on 2 May 2024.

## Relevant quotes

Section C, Electricity:

> "Electricity generation increased slightly in 2021. Electricity generated from renewable
> sources this year was 82.1 per cent of total generation, an increase from 81.1 per cent in
> 2020."

> "New Zealand generated 43,267GWh in 2021, a 0.2 per cent increase from 2020. In 2020,
> electricity generation fell as restrictions on activities and movements as part of the
> country's response to the COVID-19 pandemic led to lower electricity demand."

> "The share of renewable generation in 2021 rose to 82.1 per cent, despite La Niña weather
> conditions affecting the country. La Niña events typically bring warmer than normal
> temperatures to the whole country, along with a surge of rain to the North Island and reduced
> rainfall to the South Island."
