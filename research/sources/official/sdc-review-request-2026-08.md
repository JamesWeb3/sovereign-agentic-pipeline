## Summary

The council's report on the Southland Sustainable Resources Coalition's request to review the
consent conditions — the live challenge to the Makarewa consent, and the first document in
this corpus that names the objecting organisation and states its technical grounds. The
decision falls due 5 September 2026.

## Relevant quotes

Purpose:

> "The purpose of this report is to inform Council of a request from the Southland Sustainable
> Resources Coalition (SSRC) concerning the Datagrid Hyperscale Data Centre at Makarewa."

The request and its statutory basis:

> "On 27 July 2026, the SSRC submitted a request that Council initiate a review of the
> conditions of Resource Consent RMA/2025/53212 for the Datagrid Hyperscale Data Centre at
> Makarewa."

> "The request relies on Condition GEN4 of the consent and the review provisions in section
> 128 of the Resource Management Act (RMA) and raises concerns primarily relating to the
> acoustic assessment that supported the consent application."

The grounds, in full:

> "The request raises a range of matters relating primarily to the acoustic assessment
> undertaken in support of the consent application, including concerns regarding assessment
> methodology, low-frequency noise and infrasound, identification of noise sources, adequacy
> of baseline monitoring, the unspecified ground factor for sound propagation, and the
> sufficiency of the existing consent conditions."

Why officers are treating it seriously:

> "Given the public interest in the Datagrid consent and the potential significance of the
> matters raised, Council officers have commenced a preliminary assessment of the request and
> developed a project plan (Attachment A) to support a structured, transparent,
> evidence-based and legally robust assessment process."

What the assessment will cover:

> "The assessment will include: • a review of the resource consent application and decision
> documentation; • consideration of the matters raised by SSRC; • consultation with the
> consent holder; • obtaining legal advice, where required, regarding the scope and
> application of Council's review powers under sections 128 and 129 of the RMA; and
> • commissioning an independent acoustic review, if considered necessary."

The review window, and the council's own correction of the dates:

> "Resource consent RMA/2025/53212 was granted on 11 March 2026. Condition GEN4 enables
> Council to review the conditions of consent within six months of the decision date where the
> circumstances contemplated by section 128 of the RMA arise."

> "(Post Meeting note: It was confirmed during the meeting that the dates referenced in the
> report are incorrect and relate to the Environment Southland consents. The correct dates are
> 5 March and 5 September 2026. All parties have been advised of this correction.)"

Who decides:

> "Under Council's delegations, the decision on whether to initiate a review of the consent
> conditions may be made by the Group Manager Regulatory Services or the Manager Resource
> Consent Processing."

The recommendation put to Council was only:

> "That the Council: a) notes the information contained in the report."
