## Summary

Southland District Council's land use consent decision for the Makarewa datacentre — the
second regulator's decision, six days before Environment Southland's. It is the primary
record of the site's full extent, the built form, and the council's own one-sentence
treatment of why the application was not notified.

## Relevant quotes

The consent and the site, from the decision header:

> "RMA/2025/53212
> Datagrid New Zealand Limited Partnership
> 342 Flora Road East, Makarewa, 370 Flora Road East, Makarewa, 63 Taylor Road, Lorneville
> Lot 2 DP 526953 (RT 847503) / Lot 1 DP 595572 (RT 1149470) / Lot 2 DP 595572 (RT 1149471) /
> Lot 3 DP 595572 (RT 1149472) / Lot 10 DP 14668 (RT SL11C/835) / Lot 13 DP 14668 (RT 7092)
> General Rural
> HV Transmission Lines traverse the site
> Discretionary
> Landuse consent to establish a hyperscale datacentre including a GXP substation"

What is being built:

> "The datacentre proposal is generally comprised of the following components:
> • Six datahalls contained within three modules with a total area of approximately 9.5ha
> • Each of the six datahalls has dedicated cooling plant and back up generators
> • Cooling plant will utilise adiabatic cooling to provide efficiencies in water supply and
> power usage
> • The six datahalls will be supported by a total of 84 generators for emergency back up in
> the event of power failure to the site.
> • A cable landing station for the subsea cable.
> • Telecom infrastructure
> • A Grid Exit Point (GXP) Substation (approximately 4ha).
> • Support Buildings – logistics, security, tech service offices
> • Water treatment areas"

Consents sought, from the key aspects list:

> "• The construction of two new support towers which exceed 25m in height
> • Establishment of a datacentre
> • Establishment of a GXP substation
> • Establishment of new water treatment facilities is assessed as a discretionary activity.
> • Operation of datacentre which will breach a nighttime noise limit
> • Storage of diesel and diesel exhaust fluid on site and
> • Consent under the National Environmental Standard for Assessing and Managing Contaminants
> in Soil to Protect Human Health the change for a change in land use."

On notification — the council's entire treatment of the question:

> "Section 104(3)(d) states that consent must not be granted if an application should have
> been notified and was not. No matters have arisen in the assessment of this application
> which would indicate that the application ought to have been notified."

The decision:

> "That, for the above reasons, the application be granted pursuant to Sections 104, 104B and
> Part 2 of the Resource Management Act 1991."

Condition GEN1, fixing the application version:

> "The datacentre and associated works must be undertaken in accordance with the application
> lodged with Southland District Council (Council) on 12/09/2025, and all information
> submitted with the application as held on Council's resource consent file referenced
> RMA/2025/53212"

Condition OP7 — Potable Water Supply:

> "Potable water supply is to be provided via onsite rainwater collection and must comply with
> the Drinking Water Acceptable Solution for Roof Water Supplies from Taumata Arowai. Water
> tank storage of 150m3 is to be provided for potable water supply."

On stormwater:

> "Stormwater is to be collected via rainwater collection and controlled release to the Taylor
> Road" [wetland]

On construction traffic thresholds:

> "150 veh/hr at the SH6/Flora Road East intersection; or 180 veh/hr at the SH98/Taylor Road
> intersection; the consent holder shall update the CTMP to avoid, remedy or mitigate peak
> hour traffic effects. This may include considering additional methods to control peak hour
> vehicle movements such as carpooling, shuttles, staggering the starting and finishing times
> for staff, and other practicable travel demand measures."

On construction hours and workers:

> "This condition does not preclude the consent holder from undertaking quiet works outside
> the standard construction hours, including construction set up and pack down, and the
> arrival and departure of construction workers in light vehicles."
