## Summary

Environment Southland's public notice announcing that the resource consents Datagrid NZ
Partnership Limited sought for the Makarewa datacentre — including two groundwater takes,
a wetland removal and air discharges from diesel generators — were granted by an
independent commissioner without public notification. This is the regulator's own record
of what was consented and why it was not notified, so it is the primary source for the
water-take and non-notification claims rather than any media account of them.

## Relevant quotes

Publication line, top of notice:

> "Date: 13 Mar 2026, 1:31 pm
> Categories: Public notice"

On scope of Environment Southland's role:

> "Datagrid NZ Partnership Limited applied for resource consents associated with
> establishing and operating a datacentre near Makarewa. The resource consents which
> Environment Southland has responsibility for relate to discharges to air and land,
> taking water, and a wetland — these are listed below."
> — section "Environment Southland resource consents"

On the decision:

> "The resource consents sought by Datagrid NZ Partnership Limited from Environment
> Southland are granted. All resource consents were determined by an independent
> commissioner."
> — section "Environment Southland resource consents"

On non-notification and who was consulted:

> "The application for the consents was not publicly notified because the activities were
> assessed as having no more than minor environmental effects, as per the Resource
> Management Act. Directly affected parties were consulted and they provided written
> approvals – tāngata tiaki of the Ōreti Mātaitai, Invercargill City Council and
> Department of Conservation, and landowners with potentially affected bores."
> — section "Environment Southland resource consents"

The consents granted, verbatim list:

> "To discharge treated wastewater to land from an amenities' block
> To discharge contaminants to air from diesel generators (02)
> To take and use groundwater (03)
> To take groundwater to dewater a construction area (04)
> To remove a wetland (05)
> Occupy the coastal marine area with a datacable (06)
> Install a datacable in the coastal marine area (07)
> To discharge water from dewatering of a site (08)
> Earthworks in proximity of a wetland (09)"
> — section "Consents granted are:"

On the wetland:

> "The ecologist who reviewed the application noted that the affected wetland is small and
> dominated by exotic pasture species with no indigenous species present. No modifications
> or drainage were authorised to the regionally significant wetland at the south end of the
> site."
> — section "Environment Southland resource consents"

On capacity and the subsea cable:

> "The datacentre will provide up to 240 megawatts (MW) of IT capacity and will be utilised
> for Artificial Intelligence (AI) training, processing and data storage. To enable and
> support the datacentre a highspeed internet cable providing connectivity to the site will
> be installed. The subsea cable will extend from Ōreti Beach, Invercargill to Australia."
> — section "Environment Southland resource consents"

On the limits of the regulator's remit — relevant to the electricity-demand objection:

> "Environment Southland is not a construction or energy regulator and does not have any
> regional rules relating to the use of generated electricity."
> — section "Environment Southland resource consents"

On the separate land use consent:

> "Southland District Council issued a land use consent to establish a hyperscale
> datacentre including a GXP substation (not notified)."
> — section "Southland District Council land use consent"
