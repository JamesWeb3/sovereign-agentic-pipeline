**Plan Step:** Step 10 (Part B — pipeline) · **Lead:** James
**Status:** scaffolded only. Part B starts week 10; do not implement ahead of that.

## What this will be

Break-even analyses for running this sovereign pipeline versus paying for a hosted
commercial API, across four NZ business types:

- a **law firm**
- a **healthcare provider**
- a **local council**
- an **accounting firm**

Each case combines the measured energy cost (Step 3), NZ electricity pricing, and hardware
amortisation to find where self-hosting overtakes per-token vendor fees — the "is it worth
paying for" proof. Every number traces to a source or a calculation (docs/methodology.md),
and a case that fails to break even is reported as-is.

**Done** when all four cases have defensible break-even points with their assumptions
committed alongside.
